Privacy Policy

SquadHost Shift (com.uluer.solutions.vardiyatakip) stores shift and team data on your device. We do not operate a cloud backend for your team records.

Data on your device

  • Profile name and a random device identifier
  • Teams, memberships, and clock-in/out events

Permissions

  • Camera — scan QR/barcodes for invites and clock events
  • NFC — read or write shift tags
  • Local network — sync entries between member and leader devices (Multipeer)

Sharing

Data is shared only between devices you pair via QR invite or local sync. We do not sell or use your data for advertising.
